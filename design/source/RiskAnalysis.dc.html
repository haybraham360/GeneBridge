Carrier

You carry one copy of the sickle cell gene change (HbAS).

A risk is not a diagnosis

Sickle cell trait (you)One changed copy. Healthy, no symptoms. Can pass the copy on.
Sickle cell diseaseTwo changed copies. A lifelong condition that needs medical care.

Everyone carries something

Most people carry a few gene changes without ever knowing. Finding yours means you can plan with information instead of worry.
