Questions people ask

Does a carrier result mean I'm sick?

No. Carriers are healthy. Being a carrier only matters when planning children, and only if your partner carries a change in the same gene.

Will my partner or family see my result?

Only if you choose. Your partner sees a couple view only when you both agree, and you can tell relatives about a family risk without revealing your own result.

Will GeneBridge tell us whether to marry?

No. GeneBridge gives information and support, never a recommendation. Every option is shown equally and the decision is always yours.

Does the AI assistant interpret my result?

No. It answers general questions about genetics. Your personal result, and anything uncertain or complex, is explained by a qualified counsellor.
