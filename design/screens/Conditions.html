Beta-thalassemia

In your results
Autosomal recessiveGene HBBBlood

Reduces the body's ability to make haemoglobin. Carriers are healthy; people with two changed copies often need lifelong care.

Read moreHow it's inherited

Alpha-thalassemia

VariesGene HBA1, HBA2Blood

Affects haemoglobin production. Inheritance involves several gene copies, so a counsellor explains each family's chances.

If this runs in your family, talk to a counsellor.

Read moreHow it's inherited

Sickle cell disease

Autosomal recessiveGene HBBBlood

Red blood cells change shape, which can cause pain and anaemia. Carriers (sickle cell trait) are usually healthy.

Read moreHow it's inherited

G6PD deficiency

X-linkedGene G6PDBlood

Red blood cells can break down after certain foods or medicines. More common in males; many people never have symptoms.

If this runs in your family, talk to a counsellor.

Read moreHow it's inherited

Spinal muscular atrophy (SMA)

In your results
Autosomal recessiveGene SMN1Nerves and muscles

Affects the nerves that control muscles. Carriers are healthy. Treatments now exist.

Read moreHow it's inherited

Duchenne muscular dystrophy

X-linkedGene DMDNerves and muscles

Causes muscle weakness that gets worse over time, mainly in boys.

If this runs in your family, talk to a counsellor.

Read moreHow it's inherited

Cystic fibrosis

Autosomal recessiveGene CFTRLungs and digestion

Thick mucus affects the lungs and digestion. Carriers are healthy.

Read moreHow it's inherited

Hereditary hearing loss (GJB2)

Autosomal recessiveGene GJB2Hearing and vision

A common inherited cause of hearing loss from birth. Carriers have normal hearing.

Read moreHow it's inherited

Retinitis pigmentosa

VariesGene Several genesHearing and vision

A group of conditions that gradually affect vision. It can be inherited in different ways.

If this runs in your family, talk to a counsellor.

Read moreHow it's inherited

Phenylketonuria (PKU)

Autosomal recessiveGene PAHMetabolic

The body can't break down an amino acid in food. Managed with a special diet from birth.

Read moreHow it's inherited

Congenital adrenal hyperplasia

Autosomal recessiveGene CYP21A2Metabolic

Affects hormones made by the adrenal glands. Treatable with medicine.

Read moreHow it's inherited

Familial Mediterranean fever

Autosomal recessiveGene MEFVImmune system

Causes repeated episodes of fever and inflammation. Treatable with medicine.

Read moreHow it's inherited

Familial hypercholesterolaemia

Autosomal dominantGene LDLR and othersHeart

Causes very high cholesterol from a young age. One changed copy is enough to have it.

If this runs in your family, talk to a counsellor.

Read moreHow it's inherited

Huntington disease

Autosomal dominantGene HTTBrain

Affects the brain, usually starting in adulthood. One changed copy is enough to have it.

If this runs in your family, talk to a counsellor.

Read moreHow it's inherited

Fragile X syndrome

X-linkedGene FMR1Development

A common inherited cause of learning difficulties. Inheritance can change between generations.

If this runs in your family, talk to a counsellor.

Read moreHow it's inherited

Bardet-Biedl syndrome

Autosomal recessiveGene Several genesSeveral systems

Affects vision, kidneys, weight and other body systems. Carriers are healthy.
